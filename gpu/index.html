Read this before the numbersHow to read the snapshot

  • The price band is a range, not an index. It spans only the eligible comparable rates in that row (fixed-price Pod tiers, plus any Vast row with 5 or more matching offers). Thin-market and 8× bundle rows are never folded into it, and rates are never averaged or pooled across providers.
  • Region is source-provided or unknown. None of the three sources publish a normalized region field for these rates in this capture, so treat every row as global/unspecified unless a source states otherwise.
  • Vast is a marketplace, not a price list. Its rate is whatever independent hosts have listed at query time — it can swing from a single thin offer to dozens within the same day. RunPod and Lambda publish standing rate cards instead.
  • Availability, risk, and billing terms differ by source and are not folded into the price band. See each provider card below for what is (and isn't) known about isolation tier, support, and what the rate includes.

How this is builtMethodology / limitations

All rates shown are listed, source-specific prices — not transaction prices, and not a single settled rate for any GPU model. Vast's rate is a live marketplace offer; RunPod's and Lambda's are published rate-card prices. These are different kinds of numbers and are never pooled into a single figure.

Product tiers and configurations are not equal. Vast's row is a single verified 1× GPU instance from an independent host; RunPod's Community and Secure Pods are two distinct fixed-price products; Lambda's rate is a per-GPU price inside an 8× bundle that also includes CPU, RAM, and storage. Treat every cross-source comparison as directional, not exact.

Region is global/unknown unless a source states otherwise. None of the three captures in this snapshot carry a normalized per-row region field.

No taxes, storage, or egress are normalized into any rate. Lambda explicitly states its rates are plus applicable sales tax/VAT/GST; RunPod and Vast do not itemize taxes or egress in the captured price field.

Source prices may change at any time, and each source has its own independent capture time — see the freshness badges above and the per-provider capture date in data.json. This page does not restate any source's terms-of-service, SLA, or compliance claims beyond what each pricing page itself publishes.

This is a manually captured snapshot, not a live feed. Freshness badges flag a capture as stale past 40 days. This is a provider price-level monitor, not a blended market index and not a cheapest-GPU finder — always check the per-row product label and eligibility before citing a number.
