How this is builtMethodology

Prices are median listed offer rates from Vast.ai's on-demand marketplace, not transaction prices or a fixed price list. The published export does not contain enough raw configuration to normalize different GPU counts or storage configurations, so the unit is labelled listed offer $/hr rather than per-GPU hour.

Change since first observation is the percent change from a model's first recorded median to this snapshot. Days observed makes the comparison window explicit; new means only one snapshot exists so far.

dlperf/$ is Vast.ai's own deep-learning-performance score divided by price. It is a vendor workload score, not a universal measure of raw performance or application-specific value.

This table shows 18 of 22 tracked models. Four single-offer tiers that duplicate an adjacent price band (RTX PRO 6000 WS, RTX 4080S, RTX 4070S Ti, RTX 3080 Ti) are omitted for readability. Any row below five offers is labelled thin market and excluded from headline rankings.

Read before you citeLimitations

This is a manually captured snapshot, not a live feed. The freshness badge becomes stale after 24 hours. This public export contains rounded model-level summaries, not raw offer records or the private tracker query, so it cannot independently reproduce every change value. Several models trade on thin markets, so their medians can be noisy. Vast.ai is one marketplace among several and should not be read as the market-clearing rate everywhere.
