How this is builtMethodology

Prices are median asking rents per GPU-hour on Vast.ai's on-demand market — actual listed offers, not a fixed price list. Δ 44d is the percent change in median $/hr from the first to the most recent recorded snapshot for that model in the tracked window; new means only one snapshot exists so far, so no comparison is possible.

dlperf/$ is Vast.ai's own deep-learning-performance score divided by price — a higher number means more compute per dollar, not more raw performance.

This table shows 18 of 22 tracked models. Four low-signal, single-offer tiers that duplicate an adjacent model's price band (RTX PRO 6000 WS, RTX 4080S, RTX 4070S Ti, RTX 3080 Ti) were left out to keep the table readable; their data still exists upstream. Vast.ai itself already excludes very low offer counts from its own reporting for statistical sanity.

Read before you citeLimitations

This is a manually captured snapshot, not a live feed. It reflects one point in time (2026-09-21 10:00 UTC) pulled from a private internal tracker and republished here by hand — there is no automated refresh in this public repository. Several models trade on very thin markets (as few as 1-2 live offers), so their median/min prices can be noisy and unrepresentative of a deeper market. Vast.ai is one marketplace among several (Lambda, RunPod, CoreWeave, etc.) and its prices should not be read as the market-clearing rate everywhere.
